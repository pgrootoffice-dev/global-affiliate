Affiliate disclosure

Useful Work Tools may use affiliate links. Here is what that means for readers—and what it does not mean.

The short version

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you purchase, subscribe, or sign up through one of those links, Useful Work Tools may receive a commission. This generally does not increase the price you pay.

Affiliate compensation does not determine our editorial conclusions. It does not buy positive coverage, a higher ranking, inclusion in an article, or the removal of relevant criticism.

How affiliate links are identified

When an article contains an affiliate link, we place a clear disclosure near the relevant content. Links to official product sites are not automatically affiliate links. At launch, the editorial guides on this site use ordinary official links and no invented affiliate URLs.

How we choose what to cover

Coverage is based on the reader problem, the relevance of the product or category, available evidence, and the value of the resulting guide. Commission availability or rate is not an editorial criterion.

Commercial relationships are governed by the same accuracy, corrections, and independence standards described in our editorial policy.

Your choice

You are never required to use an affiliate link. You can navigate directly to a company’s website or locate the product independently. Before purchasing software, check the vendor’s current pricing, terms, cancellation rules, and privacy practices.

Questions

Questions about a disclosure or a specific link can be sent through our public contact channel.
