Contact

Corrections, feedback, and inquiries.

We use GitHub Issues as a public contact channel so the site does not expose a personal email address or collect information through a form.

Send a message through GitHub

Open an issue in the publication’s repository and choose a concise title. Please do not include private, confidential, financial, or sensitive personal information: issues are public.

Open a public GitHub issue

What to include

  • Corrections: the page URL, the statement in question, and a reliable source.
  • Reader feedback: the decision or workflow you would like a future guide to address.
  • Business inquiries: the company, proposed relationship, and any disclosure requirements.

Editorial boundary: product information is welcome, but submission does not guarantee coverage or a favorable conclusion. Paid placement will not be presented as independent editorial work.

If a private contact channel is added later, this page will be updated. Until then, do not post anything to GitHub that should remain confidential.
