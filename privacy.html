Privacy policy

This notice describes the site as it operates today: a static publication with no site-operated analytics, account system, contact form, or advertising cookies.

Information collected by this site

Useful Work Tools does not currently ask visitors to create an account, enter an email address, submit a form, make a payment, or provide personal information directly on the site.

We do not currently install analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or cookie-based tracking technologies. Accordingly, this site does not display a cookie consent banner.

Hosting and external services

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as IP addresses, request details, and browser information, under GitHub’s own policies.

Links may take you to third-party websites. Those services control their own data practices, and their privacy policies apply once you visit them. The public contact method uses GitHub Issues; anything posted there is public and subject to GitHub’s terms and privacy practices.

Affiliate links

Affiliate links may be added in the future. An affiliate network or merchant may use referral parameters, cookies, or similar technologies to attribute a qualifying action. We will identify affiliate relationships and update this notice if our own site practices materially change.

Changes to this policy

If we introduce analytics, forms, advertising, newsletters, or other data-collecting features, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected, why, and the choices available to visitors.
